# Shared Laundry – Interview Guide (Milestone 1)

Sep 30, 2026 · @Trần Duy Tài

## How to use this guide

The goal is to learn how people who share one washer actually handle it today, not to test our device. Aim for at least 6 tenants this week, then go back to at least 3 of them in Round 2.

**Before you start**

- Work in pairs: one person asks, one takes notes and quotes word for word.
- Plan 20–30 minutes. Interview in the flat if you can, so you can also observe the machine (AEIOU).
- Read the consent script, and record only if they agree.

> Consent script: "We're students at SUTD studying how people share laundry machines. It takes about 20 minutes. There are no right answers. We'd like to take notes and, if you're OK with it, record audio. We'll use a code like P01 instead of your name, and you can stop at any time. Is that OK?"

**Rules for good answers**

1. Ask about the last time it happened, not what they usually do or would do.
2. Follow up with "why?" or "tell me more" at least twice on anything surprising.
3. Never describe our idea or device before the last section. If they suggest a solution, ask what problem it would fix for them.
4. Stay quiet after a question. Let silence do the work.
5. Write down exact words for anything quotable.

**Writing it up (IDC interview template)**

Fill the template within 24 hours. Each box comes from these question groups:

| Template box | Filled from |
| --- | --- |
| Who, When & Where | Warm-up questions + your own notes (use the participant code, not the full name) |
| Activities | Their last laundry story, step by step |
| Environment | Where the machine is, how they communicate, what you observe |
| Likes | What works today, what they value |
| Dislikes | Pains, frustrations, conflicts |
| Quotes | Exact words, 2–4 per interview |
| Suggestions | Only ideas they raised themselves, plus the concept reaction at the end |
| Key findings | Your interpretation: insights in the form "needs X BUT Y because Z", and latent needs |

## Part A: Tenants who share a washer

For people who share one washer (and dryer) with 5 or more others. Starred questions are the must-asks if time is short.

**1. Warm-up (2 min)**

1. How many people live in your flat, and how many use the same washer? \*
2. How well do you know your housemates? Do you have a house group chat?
3. How often do you do laundry, and at what times?

**2. The last time (8 min)**

4. Tell me about the last time you did laundry, from deciding to wash to putting clothes away. \*
5. How did you know the washer was free? What did you do if it wasn't? \*
6. While it was running, where were you and what were you doing?
7. How did you know it had finished? How long until you collected it? \*

**3. Problems and conflicts (8 min)**

8. Tell me about the last time the washer was busy when you needed it. What happened?
9. Have you ever found someone else's clothes left in the machine? What did you do? \*
10. Have you ever forgotten your own laundry? Why, and what happened? \*
11. Has laundry ever caused tension with a housemate? How was it handled?
12. Of everything we've talked about, what annoys you most, and why? \*

**4. Today's workarounds (4 min)**

13. What do you or your housemates do to avoid these problems (timers, chat messages, notes, a schedule)? \*
14. What have you tried that didn't work, or that people stopped doing? Why?
15. How do you usually get messages from housemates or the landlord (WhatsApp, Telegram, other)?

**5. Wrap-up (3 min)**

16. If you could change one thing about sharing the washer, what would it be?
17. Who else should we talk to? Can we contact you again in a few days for a short follow-up?

**6. Optional concept reaction (last 2 min, only after everything above)**

Describe the idea in one neutral sentence: "a small tag on the machine that shows if it's free and whose load is inside, and messages you when it's done". Then ask:

18. What would that change for you, if anything?
19. What would stop you or your housemates from using it?

Write these answers only in the Suggestions box, never in Likes or Quotes as proof of need.

## Round 2: follow-up with the same people

Go back to at least 3 tenants from Round 1, 10 minutes each. Show them what you heard, then check it.

1. "Last time you told us \[quote\]. Is that still true? Has anything happened since?"
2. "Other people said \[insight\]. Does that match your experience?"
3. Hand them a card with 5 problems. Ask them to rank the cards from most to least annoying.
4. Role-play: "Walk me through your next laundry day, and stop me where it goes wrong."
5. "We're now focusing on \[HMW\]. Is anything important missing?"

Record what changed between rounds in the Research Log (Round = 2). The before and after slide in the pitch is built from these answers.
