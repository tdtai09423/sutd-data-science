20×4 character LCD (Fordata FDCC2004 style) · printed QR sticker · RGB light bar — live from the bot

🏠 House tester
Why a sticker? A character LCD can only show letters, so the QR code is printed on the case. Each machine's QR never changes (it opens WhatsApp with START WASHER / START DRYER), so a sticker works perfectly, and it's real: scan it with your phone camera. The RGB light bar shows the colour (green free, blue in use, amber done, purple next, red alarm). The LCD prints the 4 lines the bot sends in lcd, exactly what the ESP32 will print. Shake pretends someone pressed Start without scanning: after 5 seconds the backlight blinks, the bar flashes red and the buzzer beeps.
