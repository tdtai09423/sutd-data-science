The problem · 3

Housing doesn't communicate —
and nothing on the market fixes it.

🙈 No visibility

Tenants can't see if a machine is free, who's using it, or when it'll be done.

📞 Complaints land on the landlord

Disputes, misuse and broken machines are found late, by angry tenants.

🪙 Built for laundromats

Most laundry tech is about payment — coins, cards, another app to download, machines to rewire. Free shared laundry has nothing.

The solution

Meet LaundryBot.

A stick-on tag for every machine + a WhatsApp bot.
Scan. Queue. Get pinged. No app to download, no rewiring, no arguments.

How it works

Four steps. Zero arguments.

1

Scan the tag

Point the camera at the QR on the machine. WhatsApp opens — one tap to send.

2

Use it or book it

Free? It's yours. Busy? See your expected time and book a spot.

3

Chill in your room

Reminders 5 min before and when it's your turn. No more checking.

4

Collect & hand over

Reply DONE — the next person is pinged. Washer → dryer, same load.

Works in English and Vietnamese: “@bot ai đang giặt đồ?” → who's washing, who's next, when it's free.

Built for real life

It handles the messy parts.

🚨 Forgot to scan?

The tag feels the machine start, beeps, and the house is asked “whose is this?”. Reply MINE to claim it.

🧺 Clothes left behind

Two reminders, then the machine is freed. MOVED tells the owner their clothes are in the basket.

⏱️ Honest wait times

Expected times for everyone in line, updated when a cycle runs long.

🔗 Washer → dryer

Each load is tracked end to end — your clothes, not a random load.

📋 Records

Who used what, when; missed turns; clothes left too long. CSV export.

💬 Natural language

Ask like a human, in English or Vietnamese. No commands to learn.

Who we sell to

Not roommates. Landlords & operators.

Condo management, student housing and dorm operators, co-living brands — anyone offering complimentary shared laundry.

🛡️ Less risk

Misuse and queue-jumping are caught by the sensor, not by complaints. Idle or broken machines show up in the data.

🙋 Tenants self-serve

Residents manage their own loads, queues and reminders. Fewer calls to the front desk.

📊 Usage insight

Peak hours, loads per machine, time left uncollected — plan capacity with real numbers.
